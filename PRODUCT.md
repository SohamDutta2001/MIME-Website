# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Art Teas Tree Cafe is for Kolkata college students, theatre people, artists,
writers, readers, friend groups, smokers, and intellectually curious young
adults looking for a warm offline place to sit, talk, read, and return to.
Visitors arrive on mobile first, often from a QR code, Instagram link, search,
or a friend's recommendation, and need to understand the place quickly without
losing the feeling of a handmade cafe.

## Product Purpose

The website gives the cafe a memorable digital identity, introduces the adda
philosophy behind the space, shows the real menu, and helps people decide to
visit. Success means the site feels recognisably Bengali, artistic, intimate,
and human while staying fast, accessible, and easy for the cafe owner to update
through the menu and events data pipeline.

## Brand Personality

Warm, literary, theatrical. The voice should feel like a rainy evening
conversation over tea: specific, textured, candid, and never corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid glossy luxury cafe branding, sterile startup minimalism, generic coffee
shop templates, over-polished lifestyle stock aesthetics, and any design that
feels imported rather than rooted in Kolkata adda culture.

## Design Principles

1. Put human connection before polish.
2. Let theatre and mime shape the menu language without making the interface
   hard to scan.
3. Treat the site like a Bengali little magazine: paper, ink, margin notes,
   warmth, and controlled imperfection.
4. Keep mobile performance and readability practical for Kolkata mobile data.
5. Use real cafe content whenever available; placeholders should be easy to
   replace without redesigning the surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access across the marketing pages and menu.
Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep Bengali fragments readable with proper
font support, avoid text over low-contrast textured backgrounds, and preserve a
clear menu hierarchy for screen readers and small screens.
